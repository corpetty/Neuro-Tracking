"Continuum" is used for at least four different things in ADHD research, and they are stacked, not parallel: a **phenotype** (what rating scales measure), the **mechanisms** that generate it, its place in the **nosology** of all psychopathology, and the **etiology** underneath. This section pulls those apart, asks where the axes are independent (orthogonal), correlated (oblique), or confounded (collinear), and then maps each level to the instruments that gauge it.

It grows out of the literature survey [[literature/dimensional-continuum-adhd|Dimensional & continuum models of ADHD]] and connects back to the personal case in [[about-me/connections|Literature connections]].

## Why it matters here

The payoff is not academic. Anxiety, sleep debt and mood disturbance are *collinear* with ADHD at the phenotype — they produce near-identical surface symptoms — but they dissociate one level down, at the mechanisms. That is the concrete argument, developed in the measurement note, for why sorting out the personal `tension:adhd-vs-anxiety-internal-restlessness` needs mechanism-level, multi-method assessment rather than another rating scale.
