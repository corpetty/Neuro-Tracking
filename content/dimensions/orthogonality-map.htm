Dimensions of ADHD and their orthogonality

A map of what "dimension" means at four levels of the system — and where the axes are independent, correlated, or confounded.

"Continuum" gets used for at least four different things in ADHD research. They are stacked, not parallel: a phenotype, the mechanisms that generate it, its place in the map of all psychopathology, and the liability underneath. Orthogonality only means something within a level — and it rarely survives the jump between levels.

Orthogonal — independent axes Oblique — correlated, share variance Collinear — confounded, not separable

Four levels

1 Phenotype  what rating scales measure
InattentionHyperactivity / impulsivitySeverity / trait load
 Oblique
OrthogonalityInattention and hyperactivity/impulsivity are strongly correlated (latent r ≈ .68–.78; Arildskov 2020). Bifactor models fit best: one dominant general-ADHD factor plus two weak specific factors, the hyperactivity-specific one almost fully absorbed by the general factor. The DSM presentations are just positions in this (skewed) 2-D plane. Severity is a third, roughly independent axis.
How it's gaugedASRS v1.1, Conners / CAARS, SWAN, BAARS-IV — self and informant report.
2 Mechanism  the neurocognitive processes underneath
Response inhibitionSustained attention / RT variabilityWorking memoryReward / delay aversionTemporal processingArousal / state regulation
 Near-orthogonal
OrthogonalityThis is where real independence lives. The multiple-pathway model finds these processes are dissociable: executive/inhibitory deficits are present but neither necessary nor sufficient, and a motivational delay-aversion route runs partly independent of the inhibitory one. Different people reach the same phenotype by different mechanistic profiles (equifinality).
How it's gaugedStop-signal & go/no-go (inhibition), CPT (sustained attention, RT variability), n-back / digit span (working memory), delay discounting (reward), time reproduction (temporal), pupillometry / EEG (arousal); the Cognitive Trade-off Task.

OrthogonalityIn HiTOP, ADHD loads on the disinhibited externalizing spectrum, cross-loading to antagonism. The spectra are correlated by design — that correlation is what forces a general p-factor on top — so the axes are orthogonal only after a bifactor extraction (p ⟂ residual spectra).

4 Etiology  the liability distribution beneath
Additive polygenic liabilityRare / CNV variantsShared vs non-shared environment
 Collinear across disorders
OrthogonalityLiability is continuous with the population trait and heritability is roughly constant across its range — the backbone of the continuum claim. But it is not orthogonal across conditions: ADHD's genetic correlations with the rest of externalizing (and beyond) are high, so "the ADHD dimension" is genetically entangled with neighbours.
How it's gaugedPolygenic scores; twin and family designs (research settings only).

The crux — the cross-level map is many-to-many

Orthogonality inside a level does not carry across levels. One phenotypic axis is fed by several mechanistic ones, and one mechanism spills across several phenotypic axes — so you cannot read mechanism off phenotype. A single rating score collapses all four levels into one number.

Rating vs task is itself orthogonal

Questionnaire and performance measures of the "same" construct correlate only weakly (median r ≈ .19; Toplak 2013). Method variance dominates — so "measuring the dimension" depends on which instrument family you pick.

Mimics overlap at the mechanism level

Anxiety, sleep debt and mood disturbance share mechanism axes with ADHD (arousal, sustained attention). They look identical at the phenotype and only become separable one level down. Mechanism-level orthogonality is the discriminating lever.

Etiology ties to phenotype, continuously

Heritability is roughly constant across the trait range and clinical ADHD is the genetic extreme of the population trait — the level 1 ↔ level 4 link the taxometric and twin evidence establishes.
