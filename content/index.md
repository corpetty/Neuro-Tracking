Welcome to **Neuro-Tracking** — a personal digital garden for tracking, summarizing, and connecting scientific research in neuroscience and cognitive science.

## Sections

- [[literature/index|Literature]] — papers I'm reading, each with a structured summary.
- [[about-me/index|About Me]] — my symptoms, diagnostics, and how the research connects to my own case.
- [[graph/index|Knowledge graph]] — the live, typed graph tying it all together: sessions, symptoms, findings, mechanisms, hypotheses and the evidence behind them.

## About

This site is built with [Quartz v5](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/) and published via GitHub Pages. Source notes live in the `content/` directory of the repository. Each paper gets its own summary note in [[literature/index|Literature]], and [[about-me/index|About Me]] links that research to personal context.

Underneath the prose there's a typed knowledge graph built with [corpus-graph](https://github.com/corpetty/corpus-graph): every session, symptom, finding, mechanism, hypothesis and paper is a node, and the relations between them are declared rather than inferred. See [[graph/index|Knowledge graph]] for the live view and [[graph/ontology|the ontology]] for the vocabulary it's allowed to use.
