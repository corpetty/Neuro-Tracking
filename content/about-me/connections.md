## How to add a connection

1. Make sure the symptom exists in [[about-me/symptoms|Symptom log]] and the paper has a summary in [[literature/index|Literature]].
2. Add a row linking the two with a short description of the *possible* mechanism or relevance.
3. Note your confidence and any follow-up (further reading, a question for a clinician, a metric to check).
