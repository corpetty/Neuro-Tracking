## How I use this section

I keep the symptom log and diagnostic history current, and whenever a paper in [[literature/index|Literature]] seems relevant, I add a row to [[about-me/connections|Literature connections]] describing the possible link. Wikilinks make these relationships show up in the graph view, so clusters between my case and the research become visible over time.
